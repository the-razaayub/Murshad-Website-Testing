Desi Flavours, Made For Sharing

Welcome to Murshad Restaurant. Our menu is built around Desi Butt Karahi favourites: chicken and mutton karahi with optional pure makhan, handi and daal, charcoal BBQ, special murgh pulao and a full traditional breakfast.

Craving something different? Our Pizza Crust menu adds classic and special pizzas, burgers, wraps, starters and family deals. Browse both menus, build your order and send it on WhatsApp in a few taps.
